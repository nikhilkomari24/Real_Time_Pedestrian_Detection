About SEER

According to the report on the global state of road safety, 1.25 million deaths occur because of road accidents every year around the world. Half of the victims were identified as pedestrians, cyclists, and motorcyclists i.e. Vulnerable Road Users (VRU). The goal of the project is to develop a system to detect pedestrians on the crosswalk through live video stream and communicate the results to smart vehicles nearby.
