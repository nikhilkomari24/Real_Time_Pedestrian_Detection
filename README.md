>According to the report on the global state of road safety, 1.25 million deaths occur because of road accidents every year around the world. Almost half of the victims were identified as pedestrians, cyclists, and motorcyclists, defined collectively as Vulnerable Road Users (VRU).

>The goal of the project is to develop a system to detect pedestrians on the crosswalk through live video stream and communicate the results to smart vehicles nearby. The system will assist the drivers of smart vehicles by alerting them before reaching the crosswalk. The project aims to avoid on-road casualties caused by fast moving vehicles. The system is an enhancement for self-driving cars as it will efficiently detect obstacles even before they appear in a vehicle’s field of view.
